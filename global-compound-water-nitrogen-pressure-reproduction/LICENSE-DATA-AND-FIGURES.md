# Reuse of authors' data and figures

The authors license their original analysis results, tables, documentation,
Figure 1 artwork and original figure design under the [Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution should cite the paper and the versioned repository record once
available, and identify changes made by subsequent users.

This grant applies only to rights held by the authors. It does **not**
relicense underlying third-party datasets, boundaries or observational values.
IPCC Atlas and GRQA content retain their own CC BY 4.0 terms, Natural Earth
content is public domain, and HydroBASINS-derived identifiers and geometry
remain subject to HydroSHEDS terms. The Figure 7 composite displays
independent-source results; users should consult the original Borges and
IMAGE-GNM access terms before extracting or redistributing underlying values.
The original Borges and IMAGE-GNM files and their matched basin values are
not supplied in this archive. See `docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
